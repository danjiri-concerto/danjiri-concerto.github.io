About

Even space exploration has to be cost-effective now! MINIMUM JOURNEY is a board game about the tension of deciding how many cards to draw, and about building a deck from cards whose effects are all unique.

Players 3–4
Play time 20–40 min
Weight BGG 2.2/5.0
Price 2,000 JPY
Released Autumn 2024

Rules

Story

In the year 2XXX, a meteorite impact nearly wiped out humanity. The researchers working on space development were hit as well, and it seemed that humanity's journey into space had come to an end. One hundred years later, however, humanity has made a dramatic recovery and is enjoying peace. Scientists of the new era have begun building space exploration ships to make good use of the space technology their predecessors left behind. And so the space race has started again, with each nation's prestige on the line.

You are an astronaut entrusted with your nation's exploration ship. You must build a better ship faster than the other nations and collect relics. Take too long building your ship and another nation will get there first. But a ship with a short range will never find the most valuable relics. Which nation will put the recovered relics to use and be the first to set foot in uncharted space? Win the space race of a new era in MINIMUM JOURNEY.

Rules in a nutshell

  1. Draw cards from your deck to gain exploration power!
  2. Careful: if you draw more cards than the other players, your exploration power is reduced!
  3. Acquire stronger cards to improve your deck!
  4. The game ends when someone acquires a card from the farthest zone. The player with the most fame points wins!

Full rules

The full rules are in the English rulebook (PDF).

The rulebook includes a card list. Card text is printed in Japanese, but every effect is also shown with icons, so the cards become easy to read after a few plays.

Errata

Game Market 2025 Autumn / 2026 Spring edition

No known errors at this time.

Game Market 2024 Autumn edition

The rulebook and some cards distributed at Game Market 2024 Autumn contain errors.

Please check the items marked [Important] before you play.

  • [Important] Number of cards to set out with 4 players (p.1, bottom left)
    • (Wrong) The number of cards revealed from each deck is as follows.
      Players \ Cost 2 4 6 8
      3 players 3 3 2 2
      4 players 3 3 2 2
    • (Correct) The number of cards revealed from each deck is as follows.
      Players \ Cost 2 4 6 8
      3 players 3 3 2 2
      4 players 4 3 2 2
  • [Important] Example of amulet ownership (p.2, bottom right; corrected on the errata sheet)
    • (Wrong) In the example above, two players, Bryan and Koki, have the lowest exploration output. Bryan explored later, so ownership of the amulet card moves to Bryan.
    • (Correct) In the example above, Alice has the lowest exploration output, so ownership of the amulet card moves to Alice. If Bryan and Koki, both with exploration output 6, had been tied for the lowest, ownership would move to Bryan, because Bryan explored later.
  • Tips and hints (p.3, bottom left) — a typo in the Japanese text only; the meaning is unchanged.
  • Credits and acknowledgements (p.3, bottom right): the name "Hogemochi" should read "Pochimochi".

  • [Important] No. 057 "航路安全のお守り" (Amulet of Safe Voyage): card effect
    • (Wrong) (no text)
    • (Correct) In round 1, treat this card's exploration power as 0.
  • [Important] No. 057 "航路安全のお守り" (Amulet of Safe Voyage): timing
    • (Wrong) (none)
    • (Correct) Exploration power change
  • No. 007 "レーザー照準器" (Laser Sight): card effect
    • (Wrong) For the rest of this round, no other player may explore that card.
    • (Correct) For the rest of this round, no other player may acquire that card.
  • No. 018 "ギャンブルチャージャー" (Gamble Charger): card name — a typo in the Japanese name only.
  • No. 032 "人力発電機" (Human-Powered Generator): timing
    • (Wrong) Exploration power change
    • (Correct) Exploration power change, fame change
  • No. 033 "太陽光発電設備" (Solar Power Plant): timing
    • (Wrong) Exploration power change
    • (Correct) Exploration power change, fame change
  • No. 040 "先駆者の道標" (Pioneer's Signpost): timing
    • (Wrong) When drawn
    • (Correct) When drawn, when acquired
